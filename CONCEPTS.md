# Concepts

Shared domain vocabulary for this project: entities, named processes, and status concepts with project-specific meaning. Seeded with the terms known at bootstrap; it grows as ce-compound and ce-compound-refresh process learnings, and direct edits are fine. Glossary only, not a spec.

## Giveaways

### Giveaway

A prize draw the bot runs for the audience of the @read_wine Telegram channel. One giveaway is bound to one channel post: a channel admin starts it with `/giveaway` and a forward of the post, and the bot attaches a join button to that post. Winner selection is not defined yet.

### Participant

A Telegram user who tapped a giveaway's join button. A user participates in a giveaway at most once.

### Channel

The @read_wine Telegram channel the bot serves. Its audience takes part in giveaways.
